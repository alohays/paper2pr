# Visual Audit: tta-2026
**Date:** 2026-08-29 | **Profile:** invited-talk | **Audience:** practitioner (deck.yml `audience.assumes: practitioner`), in-person, 40 min
**Budget applied:** bullets_max 5 / bullets_max_with_figure 3 / bullets_max_two_line 1 / box_density 1 / max_nesting 1 (from `scripts/deckprofile.py tta-2026`)

Method: full source read of `Quarto/talks/tta-2026.qmd` (514 lines, 47 rendered
slides) plus a fresh `scripts/shoot_slides.py` run at 1280x720; every slide was
inspected on rendered pixels. Read-only audit; no files edited.

## Verified clean

- **Density:** every bullet slide is within budget. Max observed: 4 bullets
  (bio, A1-A4, A6-A8) against bullets_max 5; figure slides carry at most 2
  bullets against bullets_max_with_figure 3; no slide exceeds the two-line
  allowance of 1; list nesting is flat (0 sub-levels against max_nesting 1).
- **Boxes:** zero methodbox/keybox/highlightbox/resultbox/quotebox in the deck,
  against box_density 1. (See the chip judgment call under Low findings.)
- **Fonts:** no `.smaller`/`.smallest` anywhere; titles consistent at theme
  size; no inline font-size shrink applied to body bullets or tables.
- **Centering:** no `{.top-align}`, no `{.left}`, no manual top margins;
  `auto-stretch: false` set in `Quarto/_quarto.yml` and not overridden by the
  deck.
- **Theme classes:** every class used either exists in
  `Quarto/clean-academic.scss` (.divider, .divider-number/-title/-sub,
  .video-full, .two-up, .footnote, .timeline, .tl-*, .qr-slide, .qr-block,
  .r-stretch) or is defined in the deck's own inline `<style>` block
  (.vf-panel, .vf-head, .vf-chips, .chip, .chips, .chip-light). No phantom
  classes.
- **Media paths:** all 21 video slugs referenced via `{{< video-card >}}` /
  `{.video-full video="@..."}` exist in `Figures/talks/tta-2026/videos.json`
  and on disk (33 clips + posters present). Both static figures
  (`robottt-ctx-scaling.svg`, `deck-qr.svg`) exist, are SVG (no PDF images),
  and are listed in `figures.yml`; the third-party chart carries its
  attribution footnote on slide 29. The `../../Figures/...` references resolve
  after deployment: `scripts/assemble_site.sh` copies `Figures/*` to the site
  root (pruning `videos/`), matching the two-level `slides/talks/` depth.
- **Pedagogy/structure:** dividers at every conceptual pivot (01-04, an
  interlude, Appendix); definition slides (4, 10, 19) lead each section before
  detail; the distilled takeaway slide (35) follows the dense scoreboard.

## Findings

### Slide: "A5. Skild S1: ground-up in-context learning" (slide 44)
- **Issue:** Text overflow, confirmed on rendered pixels. The two-up video
  pair plus two bullets do not fit: the second bullet wraps and its second
  line ("task") is clipped at the bottom edge of the 720px canvas. The first
  bullet also wraps ("Pretraining scaled 1k to 100k hours; at maximum
  perturbation, degradation one third of a VLA's", ~95 chars), so the slide
  spends two two-line bullets against this deck's two-line allowance of 1 -
  and physically runs off the slide.
- **Severity:** High
- **Recommendation:** Split-first. Either (a) split A5 into two appendix
  slides - the two-up video pair with one short bullet, then a text-only
  slide with the scaling/long-horizon detail - or (b) tighten both bullets to
  one line each ("Degradation at max perturbation: one third of a VLA's" /
  "One egocentric video drives a 10-minute unseen task") and move the
  qualifiers to speaker notes. Do not shrink fonts.
- **Quarto note:** for (a), a continuation slide `## A5. Skild S1: the
  evidence` keeps the appendix numbering readable; for (b), `::: {.notes}`
  holds the trimmed context.

### Slide: "The race is for hours, not parameters" (slide 26)
- **Issue:** Near-overflow. All five "What" cells wrap to two lines, pushing
  the table's last line ("still not enough") to within ~25px of the footnote
  strip; the table's bottom rule visually merges with the footnote zone. No
  clipping yet, but zero breathing room - one more word in any cell clips.
- **Severity:** Medium
- **Recommendation:** Shorten the "What" cells to one line each (e.g. "human
  ego video, no robot data", "134,806 bimanual episodes") - the fine print
  already lives in A7. Alternatively split: the four vendor rows on this
  slide, and the China/WRC row plus the Curse-of-Precision counterweight as
  their own short slide (it is arguably a second core message).
- **Quarto note:** `: {tbl-colwidths="[...]"}` (already used on slide 35)
  would widen the "What" column and stop the wrapping if the cells must stay.

### Slide: "Five architectures, one scoreboard" (slide 34)
- **Issue:** Density at the ceiling: a 5x4 table filling the full content
  height, bottom rule abutting a long two-clause quoted footnote (compute
  comparison + latency + "hybrid will dominate"). Renders without clipping,
  but the slide carries two messages: the architecture comparison and the
  NVIDIA compute/latency claim.
- **Severity:** Medium
- **Recommendation:** The distilled version already exists as slide 35 ("If
  you remember one slide"). Trim this one to keep a single message: move the
  footnote's compute/latency claim to speaker notes or promote it to its own
  brief slide; the "hybrid: the predicted winner" row then lands harder.
- **Quarto note:** `::: {.notes}` for the NVIDIA numbers keeps them available
  for Q&A without crowding the scoreboard.

### Slide: "Yunsung Lee" (slide 2) - deck-local chip CSS
- **Issue:** The inline `<style>` block defines the .vf-panel/.vf-chips/.chip/
  .chips/.chip-light system: absolute positioning over full-bleed video and
  chip body text at 0.42em (~17px rendered at 720p). The absolute positioning
  is justified (full-bleed video slides have no flow to center in), and the
  chips render consistently on ~12 slides - but 17px body text is small from
  the back row of a hall, and the pattern is a strong theme-promotion
  candidate rather than per-deck CSS.
- **Severity:** Low
- **Recommendation:** Keep for this talk (venue check: read the chip
  sub-lines from 5m at 50% zoom). Longer term, promote the chip strip into
  `clean-academic.scss` so future decks inherit one tested size.
- **Quarto note:** on the box_density budget of 1 - I read the chip strip as
  a single figure-annotation unit, not as semantic callout boxes, so no
  violation is charged; if the team reads each chip as a box, ~12 slides
  would exceed the budget and the call should be made once, in the theme.

### Slide: "{.video-full @dyna2-hero}" (slide 24) - white-on-white overlay
- **Issue:** At capture time the background clip's first frame is near-white
  and the white .vf-head ("Dyna-2: a million hours...") survives only via its
  text-shadow; the same pattern is borderline on the Genie 3 slide (white
  wall footage). Live playback darkens both, but the first-second impression
  is washed out.
- **Severity:** Low
- **Recommendation:** Retrim the clip start by ~1s or pick a darker poster
  frame in `videos.yml`; no layout change needed.

### Slide: "Where does action supervision come from?" (slide 21)
- **Issue:** The LAPA video card's opening frame is an almost-blank white
  title card, so the slide initially shows an empty white rectangle where the
  figure should be. Bullets are within budget (2 with figure, 1 wrapped).
- **Severity:** Low
- **Recommendation:** Trim the clip's in-point past the title card or set a
  representative poster frame.

### Slide: "A VLA is a vision-language model that outputs robot actions" (slide 4)
- **Issue:** The only content slide whose title wraps to two lines; every
  other content title is one line. Purely a consistency nit - it renders fine.
- **Severity:** Low
- **Recommendation:** Optional: "A VLA maps pixels and language to actions"
  or similar one-liner; the first bullet already carries the full definition.

### Slide: "Dyna-2 is deployed, not demoed" (slide 25)
- **Issue:** The "blackout" clip is a near-black rectangle when paused (by
  design - the caption explains it), and the footnote sits ~8px below the
  chip strip. No overlap; tightest chip/footnote clearance in the deck.
- **Severity:** Low
- **Recommendation:** No change required; if the footnote ever grows a second
  line, move "1 TB per day" into a chip and drop the footnote.

### Slide: divider "What does a million hours buy?" (slide 27)
- **Issue:** This divider and the Appendix divider omit `.divider-number`
  while sections carry 01-04. Reads as an intentional interlude; noted only
  so the inconsistency is a decision, not an accident.
- **Severity:** Low
- **Recommendation:** Keep as is if the interlude framing is deliberate.
