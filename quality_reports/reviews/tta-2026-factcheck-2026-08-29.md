## Mismatches

### Issue 1: ZFLOPs comparison inverted - the 50+ figure belongs to the WAM path, not the VLA path
- **Slide:** "Five architectures, one scoreboard" (footnote)
- **Severity:** CRITICAL
- **Slide says:** "NVIDIA, Jun 2026: WAM to frontier took 9 ZFLOPs vs 50+ for the VLA path; inference 3 to 4x slower, closing fast; 'hybrid will dominate.'"
- **Source says:** "DreamZero-style action tuning requires roughly 9 ZFLOPs" and "full video pretraining plus adaptation can exceed 50 ZFLOPs" - both on the WAM side. The VLA path is "~6.9 ZFLOPs for the small from-scratch VLA Foundry LLM->VLM->VLA path" and MolmoAct2 at "~9.8 ZFLOP-equivalent" (NVIDIA developer blog, Jun 15, 2026).
- **Fix:** Rewrite as: "action tuning on a pretrained world model: ~9 ZFLOPs; the full WAM pipeline with video pretraining: 50+; VLA paths: ~7-10 ZFLOPs." As written the slide claims WAM is ~5x cheaper than VLA when the source says the WAM full pipeline is the expensive one.

### Issue 2: GEN-1.5 weight-update fraction contradicts the source by 1-2 orders of magnitude
- **Slide:** A4 "GEN-1.5: lineage and evidence"
- **Severity:** CRITICAL
- **Slide says:** "updates touch 2.5 to 11.4% of weights"
- **Source says:** "Ten steps change the model weights on held out tasks by less than 0.15%" (generalistai.com/blog/gen-1.5; verified in two independent passes)
- **Fix:** Replace with "10 gradient steps change the weights by less than 0.15%". The 2.5-11.4% range appears nowhere on the page.

### Issue 3: RoboTTT assembly score is 79%, not 89%
- **Slide:** A3 "RoboTTT: the mechanism"
- **Severity:** MAJOR
- **Slide says:** "Assembly: 89% vs 42%"
- **Source says:** "RoboTTT achieves an average task completion score of 79%, 87% higher than the single-step context baseline GR00T N1.7 (42%)" (arXiv 2607.15275 HTML, quoted verbatim; 42 x 1.87 = 78.5, so 79% is the self-consistent value). One fetch of the project page reported 89% from Figure 1, but the paper's verbatim sentence and the arithmetic both say 79%.
- **Fix:** 79% vs 42% (or quote the +87% relative improvement, which the abstract also states).

### Issue 4: Gemini Robotics 2 chip fuses two different claims
- **Slide:** π0.7 video-full slide ("2026: VLAs began absorbing world models"), third chip
- **Severity:** MAJOR
- **Slide says:** "GR 2: whole-body humanoid, adapts with under 200 demos"

- **Fix:** Split or reword, e.g. "GR 2: whole-body humanoid control; its on-device model adapts to new bi-arm robots with under 200 demos." Note also the GR2 post never mentions world models, so it sits oddly under this slide's heading.

### Issue 5: GE-Sim 2.0 does not offer "teleop inside the model"
- **Slide:** "Everyone is pointing world models at robot training" (GE-Sim 2.0 row)
- **Severity:** MAJOR
- **Slide says:** "one world model, three jobs | evaluation, RL, and teleop inside the model"

Teleoperation appears only as a *training data source* for the simulator, not as a capability inside it (arXiv 2605.27491).
- **Fix:** "evaluation, reward signals for closed-loop training, accelerated rollouts."
