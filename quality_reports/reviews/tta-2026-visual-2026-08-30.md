# Visual Audit: tta-2026
**Date:** 2026-08-30 | **Profile:** invited-talk | **Audience:** TTA standardization practitioners (`audience.assumes: practitioner`), in person
**Budget applied:** **5 / 3 / 1 / 1 / 1** — 5 bullets; 3 bullets when a figure shares the slide; 1 two-line bullet; 1 colored box; 1 nested level

Step 0 was rerun with `python3 scripts/deckprofile.py tta-2026` and the deck
configuration was reread before the audit. Scope: all 851 source lines in
`Quarto/talks/tta-2026.qmd` and all 47 supplied 1280x720 captures in
`/tmp/tta-2026-shots.jtDPUa`. Capture 00 is the Quarto title; captures 01-46
are the 46 authored `##` slides. Capture 38 is the Appendix divider and
captures 39-46 are A1-A8.

## Verdict

- **No geometric overflow or clipping was found.** Every title, table, bullet,
  caption, QR code, and footer remains inside the 1280x720 frame. The apparent
  left-edge title risk on slide 6 disappears on the original-resolution image:
  it has the normal 56px inset.
- **Bullet and nesting budgets pass.** The maximum is four bullets on slide 1,
  with one wrapped bullet (effective cap four). Figure slides carry at most two
  bullets against the figure budget of three; no slide has more than one
  wrapped bullet; all lists are flat.
- **Media and classes render.** Every referenced video, the RoboTTT SVG, and the
  deck QR appear in the captures. No PDF figure is referenced. No missing or
  undefined class was found. `auto-stretch: false` remains inherited from the
  project; the deck uses neither `{.top-align}` nor `{.left}`.
- **One systemic High finding survives:** deck-local KPI chips use 0.42em body
  text and form two to four independently filled boxes on 17 slides. The
  rendered composition is consistent, but consistency does not make 17px
  explanatory copy readable from the back of an in-person room.

## Findings

### Slides using the deck-local metric-chip system (slides 5, 7, 11, 13, 15, 19, 23, 24, 28-31, 35, and 40-43)

- **Issue:** QMD lines 28 and 32 set `.chip` and `.chip-light` to
  `font-size: 0.42em`, approximately 16.8px at the 40px root. Even the bold
  metric is only `0.42 x 1.75 = 0.735em`, approximately 29.4px. The explanatory
  line carries the meaning of each number, so it is not disposable metadata.
  Each chip also has its own fill and border; the visible result is two to four
  colored boxes against this deck's box budget of one.
- **Severity:** High
- **Counterargument considered:** Round 1 treated a complete chip row as one
  figure-annotation unit, and the automated gate intentionally counts only
  semantic theme boxes. That explains why the slides look orderly and why the
  gate passes. The finding still survives because the independent fills and
  borders remain visually distinct, and the 0.42em text is an explicit
  new-deck no-shrink violation regardless of box-count interpretation.

### Slides with hand-built SVG labels (slides 3, 9, 18, and 21)

- **Issue:** The architecture diagrams use fixed 13.5-19px SVG labels (QMD
  lines 73-93, 198-216, 358-378, and 422-442). The boxes and arrows are clear,
  but the gold auxiliary phrases and gray captions are visibly much smaller
  than the bullets at 1280x720. Slide 21 is the tightest case: “two models, two
  trainings” and the inference note are approximately 14px.
- **Severity:** Medium

| Slide | Title | Specific recommendation |
|------:|-------|-------------------------|
| 3 | A VLA is a vision-language model that outputs robot actions | Remove the tiny “imitation” edge label or enlarge all diagram labels to room scale; keep the two bullets unchanged. |
| 9 | A world model learns what happens next | Shorten the rollout sentence so the diagram can use larger labels. |
| 18 | A WAM denoises video and action together | Promote “video prediction = action supervision” into the large bullet and remove the tiny edge annotation. |
| 21 | From two stages to one joint model | Split “2024” and “2026” into progressive slides, or remove auxiliary captions and enlarge the remaining labels. |

- **Quarto note:** Use a shared diagram class with theme variables and a tested
  minimum label size instead of repeating hard-coded pixel sizes and palette
  hexes. Do not reduce surrounding body type.

### Slide: "A1. Dyna-2: the scaling law in detail" (slide 39)

- **Issue:** The chart's axes, ticks, gold annotation, and point labels are
  fixed at 13-14px (QMD lines 741-765). The chart is the evidence on this
  backup slide, yet those labels are not reliably readable at classroom
  distance. It is emitted as `.diagram`, so it bypasses the theme's
  `figure.chart-figure > svg.chart` back-row chart sizing.
- **Severity:** Medium
- **Recommendation:** Rebuild the chart with at least 18-20px rendered labels
  and a larger plotting area. If vertical space becomes tight, move the
  sampler-latency bullet to an A1 continuation slide rather than shrinking it.
- **Quarto note:** Use `figure.chart-figure` and `svg.chart`, with theme color
  variables instead of hard-coded hex values.

### Slides carrying substantive takeaways in footnote type (slides 4, 6, 22, and 33)

- **Issue:** The theme renders `.footnote` at 0.45em, approximately 18px. That
  size is appropriate for source attribution, but these four footers carry
  claims the audience is expected to understand. The claims therefore become
  visually inaccessible even though none is clipped.
- **Severity:** Medium

| Slide | Title | Substantive footer evidence | Specific recommendation |
|------:|-------|-----------------------------|-------------------------|
| 4 | Three years of the same recipe, scaled | “the axis of progress stayed the same” | Promote this to one large sentence under the timeline or a statement follow-on. |
| 6 | Every VLA stands on human demonstration | “an hour of robot time buys one hour of one embodiment's data” | Make the bottleneck the main statement, or move it to a dedicated transition slide before slide 7. |
| 22 | The architecture consolidated in 2026 | training-time scaffolding and VLA-class latency | Promote the conclusion to a short statement slide; keep the three-row taxonomy separate. |
| 33 | Five architectures, one scoreboard | compute and inference cost caveat | Move the caveat to speaker notes or an existing backup slide; preserve the scoreboard's VLA/WAM comparison. |

- **Quarto note:** Use `[...]{.statement}` for a standalone takeaway or
  `::: {.notes}` for presenter-only qualification. Do not enlarge the footer
  inside an already full slide; split first.

### Slide: "Dyna-2 is deployed, not demoed" (slide 24)

- **Issue:** The captured video/poster area is effectively black. Pixel review
  measured mean luminance at approximately 7.5/255, with 99.5% of the media
  region below luminance 35. Darkness is the point of the clip, but the paused
  and PDF fallback communicates no visible chopping action on a projector.
- **Severity:** Medium
- **Recommendation:** Select a later poster/in-point that retains the blackout
  while exposing a readable robot or blade silhouette, then verify the PDF
  fallback. A brief lit comparison frame is preferable if no dark frame is
  legible.
- **Quarto note:** Adjust the manifest segment start/poster source, rerun
  `media_prep.py`, and inspect the generated poster in both HTML and the PDF;
  do not compensate with overlaid explanatory text.
