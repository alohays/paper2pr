### Critical Issues (Immediate Action Required)

1. **Fact - ZFLOPs comparison inverted** ("Five architectures" footnote).
   Slide says WAM-to-frontier took 9 ZFLOPs vs 50+ for the VLA path. The
   NVIDIA blog attributes both numbers to the WAM side (9 = DreamZero-style
   action tuning, 50+ = the full WAM video-pretraining pipeline); the VLA
   path is ~6.9-9.8 ZFLOPs. As written, the slide reverses the source's
   cost comparison - and it is the deck's central compute argument.
2. **Fact - GEN-1.5 weight-update fraction** (A4). Slide: "updates touch
   2.5 to 11.4% of weights." Source: 10 gradient steps change weights "by
   less than 0.15%." Off by 1-2 orders of magnitude.
3. **Forbidden-term near-miss** - "20+ company Cosmos coalition in Japan"
   is a direct synonym of the banned "consortium", and the claim is also
   unsourced. Cutting the line resolves both.
4. **Render + layout - A5 "Skild S1" slide overflows** (shot index 43;
   auditor slide 44 - same slide). The wrapped final word "task" is clipped
   at the bottom edge; the slide also spends two two-line bullets against
   the profile's allowance of 1. Shorten the bullets or split the slide,
   then reshoot. Slide 36 uses the same layout and sits one wording change
   from the same failure.
5. **Pedagogy - the missing "why".** Divider 01 promises "where it
   strains," but no slide states the teleop-data bottleneck the entire
   world-model/WAM/human-video arc is answering. One added line on slide 7
   fixes the narrative.
6. **Fabricated quotation** - "hybrid will dominate" is typeset as a
   verbatim quote but appears in no declared source (fact check issue 6;
   graded MAJOR there, raised here because a fabricated quote on a
   photographable slide is presentation-blocking in spirit).

### Medium Issues (Next Revision)

- **Unsourced claim clusters** (fact check U1-U6): Dyna-2 deployment and
  business metrics (Din Tai Fung, napkins/hour, 3-day ROI, 1 TB/day);
  RoboArena Elo 1750/1622 (used twice); the Cosmos 3 adopter list;
  Lightwheel Open100K details; GEN-0/GEN-1 hours and 9,000 end-effectors;
  WRC 2026 "90 data factories" and UBTech 566M CNY. Source them, soften
  them, or cut them.
- **Attribution mismatches**: RoboTTT assembly is 79% not 89%; GR2's
  "<200 demos" belongs to on-device bi-arm adaptation, not the whole-body
  humanoid; GE-Sim 2.0 has no "teleop inside the model"; Project Genie
  "280B images" and "physics gaps self-stated" are not in the source;
  "Isaac-ready" appears in neither World Labs post; the Helix clip is
  labeled autonomous though the page never says so.
- **"GR 2" chip is an undefined abbreviation** (flagged independently by
  proofreader and pedagogy): a practitioner room can misread it as
  Fourier/ByteDance GR-2, or mis-bind it to GR00T which also appears in
  the deck. Spell out "Gemini Robotics 2".
- **Slide 26 near-overflow and split message** ("race is for hours" /
  million-hours table): wrapped cells nearly touch the footnote strip;
  shorten cells or split the slide. Slide 34's full-height table plus long
  compute footnote carries two messages - move the NVIDIA claim to notes.
- **Contrast**: white titles over near-white footage on the Genie 3 slide
  (and borderline on slide 29); a dark scrim band behind full-bleed titles
  fixes both. Slide 26's top-anchored title over a mostly empty dark slide
  breaks the divider pattern and can read as broken.
- **Undefined formula**: `log N ∝ 1/(P−c)` appears twice with N, P, c
  never defined - the deck's only counterargument to the data race.
- **Zero fragment reveals**: the three best punchlines (slide 26 "still
  not enough", slide 31 66% vs 9%, slide 34 hybrid takeaway) all fire
  without build-up.
- **Wording precision** (proofreader): "+62% pretraining at 8K vs 1K
  context" never names the metric; "1.55x success of its own VLA twin" is
  garbled ("1.55x the success of its VLA twin").
- **Speaker notes missing**: `notes: ko` is declared but the committed QMD
  has zero notes blocks; with six dense tables in the back half of a
  22-minute speaking budget, run /write-speaker-notes before the talk.

### Recommended Next Steps

1. Fix the two wrong facts (ZFLOPs inversion, GEN-1.5 fraction), delete or
   reword the "coalition" line, and de-quote "hybrid will dominate" - these
   four are what cap the score.
2. Repair the A5 Skild S1 overflow (and trim slide 36's sibling layout),
   then reshoot those slides to confirm.
3. Sweep the unsourced clusters: add sources to deck.yml where they exist,
   soften to "reported" where they do not, cut the rest.
4. Add the one-line teleop-bottleneck statement to slide 7 and expand
   "GR 2" to "Gemini Robotics 2".
5. Re-run /slide-excellence (or at minimum the fact check and render audit)
   after the fixes; then generate the Korean speaker notes.
