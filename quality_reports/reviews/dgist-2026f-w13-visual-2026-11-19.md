### Resolved major · slide 12 · The illustrated load disappeared

In the first classroom and student renders, both arm drawings ended in empty space, while their caption described the weight of the same parcel. The initial screenshot showed neither parcel nor either force arrow. The root SVG and its reusable parcel symbol both used the ID `w13-shelf-load`. Each `use` resolved to the root and failed to paint the referenced geometry. The source repair assigns a unique ID to the parcel group and updates both references. After the rebuild, Chrome displayed two matching parcels and equal downward arrows on both ports at both widths. The reference-group bounding boxes measured 55 × 122 px at full width and 39 × 85 px at caption-room width. Both rulers now terminate beneath the visible parcels. No open major or blocking visual findings remain.
