# Visual Audit: dgist-2026f-w02
**Date:** 2026-08-30 | **Profile:** lecture | **Audience:** 93 first- and second-year undergraduates outside AI-related majors; assumes no prior AI knowledge; in person
**Budget applied:** 4 bullets / 3 with a figure / 1 two-line bullet / 1 colored box / 1 nesting level

## Result

The audit covered all 47 logical slides at 1280x720, including every final fragment state. There are four High-severity findings and two Medium-severity findings. The High findings should be fixed before delivery because visible content is clipped or the lecture density budget is materially exceeded.

Rendered-bound checks were used alongside the screenshots. The deck has no `.smaller` or `.smallest` use, no inline sub-1em font override, no `auto-stretch` override, no unnecessary `.top-align` or `.left`, no missing custom theme class, and no box-count violation. All 30 directly referenced local figure paths exist, the series and video assets render, and no PDF is used as an inline image.

### Slide: "Autonomous or teleoperated?" (slide 9)
- **Issue:** The final fragment state stacks three statements above a 768x432 video card. The section scroll height reaches 882px; the card ends at y=850 on a 720px canvas. Roughly 130px of the card, including most of its caption strip, is clipped. The screenshot shows only the beginning of the metadata at the bottom edge.
- **Severity:** High
- **Recommendation:** Split the reveal from the evidence. Keep the autonomy claims on the first slide, then give the teleoperation clip its own slide so the full frame and attribution remain visible. Do not shrink the video or text to preserve the current composition.
- **Quarto note:** Use a continuation slide with the same title, or make the clip a `## {.video-full video="@s08c-unitree-embodied-avatar-teleop"}` slide with its manifest caption.

### Slide: "2018-2020 · GPT: One Simple Game" (slide 16)
- **Issue:** In the final fragment state, the closing sentence occupies y=680-736, so its bottom 16px is outside the canvas and it collides with the pinned source line. The slide also asks the prompt, reveals the answer, shows a long sample box, and adds the truth warning in one frame. The single colored box is within the box budget of 1; the failure is vertical density.
- **Severity:** High
- **Recommendation:** Split after the answer. Let one slide run the next-word interaction, then use a second slide for the GPT-2 continuation and the "fluent is not true" lesson.
- **Quarto note:** Reuse the title with a progressive subtitle such as "GPT-2 continues"; keep the warning as a plain statement or the one allowed box on the second slide.

### Slide: "Foundation Models: Train Once, Do Many Things" (slide 20)
- **Issue:** The slide has 3 bullets beside a figure, exactly the nominal figure budget of 3, but all three wrap: the first is 3 lines and the other two are 2 lines each. This exceeds the allowance of 1 two-line bullet, violates the rule that a 3-line bullet is never allowed, and exceeds the effective figure budget because each wrapped bullet costs a slot.
- **Severity:** High
- **Recommendation:** Split into a pipeline slide and a definition/takeaway slide. On the first, let the figure carry the recipe with one framing sentence. On the second, define "foundation model" and state why it matters for the course.
- **Quarto note:** Use the same title on the continuation or an untitled centered follow-on. Do not reduce the font size; the current wrapping is the signal that the slide must split.

### Slide: "Two Waves, One Wall" (slide 22)
- **Issue:** The raw 2x2 grid contains two wave headings, four images, four captions, a conclusion box, and a source line. The section scroll height is 906px. The lower figure captions end at y=728, and the key box sits at y=752-874, entirely below the 720px frame; the final conclusion is therefore invisible. The box count itself is 1 against the budget of 1.
- **Severity:** High
- **Recommendation:** Split by wave, then land the shared limitation as a separate conclusion or as the final statement on the second wave slide. Each wave should show no more than the evidence needed to distinguish its learning method.
- **Quarto note:** Replace the raw 2x2 HTML grid with separate Quarto slides and native columns only where the two examples are genuinely paired. Reserve the key box for the visible "one wall" conclusion.

### Slide: "Same recipe, new output" (slide 25)
- **Issue:** The three bullets fit the figure budget of 3, but the central SVG is too finely lettered for the 93-person room. At the theme's 64% chart width, the SVG's 14px `INPUT`/`OUTPUT` labels render at roughly 10px, while its 22-25px phrases and model names render at roughly 15-18px. Those labels are the substance of the LM/VLM/VLA comparison and are not readable from the back row.
- **Severity:** Medium
- **Recommendation:** Split the diagram and verbal explanation. Give the diagram a near-full-width slide with larger internal lettering, then place the three one-line takeaways on a continuation slide.
- **Quarto note:** On the diagram slide, use a normal Quarto image at about 90% width (or enlarge the SVG's own labels) rather than shrinking any slide text.

### Slide: "The pull now comes from industry, not academia" (slide 38)
- **Issue:** The three body statements remain in frame, but the single-line source footer is wider than its container: 1230px of content against 1168px available. Its last source is clipped and visually runs into the `38 / 47` slide number. This makes required attribution harder to verify.
- **Severity:** Medium
- **Recommendation:** Split the evidence into two slides or shorten each on-slide citation to a concise source label while retaining complete bibliographic detail elsewhere. Keep an untruncated source line on every slide that carries the corresponding claim.
- **Quarto note:** Two slides with the same title and shorter `.footnote` blocks preserve the pinned-footnote layout; do not reduce the footnote font.

## Slides without findings

No additional density, overflow, typography, box-fatigue, centering, figure, or path issue was found on slides 1-8, 10-15, 17-19, 21, 23-24, 26-37, or 39-47.
