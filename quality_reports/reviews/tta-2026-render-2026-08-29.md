# Render Audit: talks/tta-2026 (2026-08-29)

- Deck: `talks/tta-2026` (invited-talk profile, 40 min, in-person)
- Input: 47 full-deck screenshots, `/tmp/tta-2026-shots/tta-2026-00.png` through `tta-2026-46.png`
- Slide numbers below use the `-NN` file index (00-46). The on-slide page counter is NN+1.
- Scope: READ-ONLY visual pass. Overflow, missing posters, legibility, broken layout, theme escapes. No source edits, no re-render.

## Verdict

The deck renders cleanly overall: every video slide shows a real poster frame (no black frames anywhere), the light academic theme is consistent, tables and stat tiles are legible from distance. One CRITICAL text overflow on slide 43, two MEDIUM issues, and eight LOW observations.

Totals: **1 CRITICAL, 2 MEDIUM, 8 LOW.**

## Findings

### CRITICAL

**Slide 43 — text clipped at the bottom edge** (`tta-2026-43.png`, "A5. Skild S1: ground-up in-context learning")
- The second bullet, "Long-horizon: a single egocentric video drives a 10-minute unseen task", wraps to a second line and the final word "task" is cut off by the bottom slide edge — only the top sliver of the word is visible above the progress bar. A person in any row sees a visibly amputated sentence.
- Cause pattern: this is the video-plus-bullets layout (two side-by-side posters with captions, then bullets), and the first bullet also wraps to two lines, pushing the block past the content area. Slide 36 uses the same layout and lands just inside the edge; slide 43 does not.
- Recommendation: shorten one of the two bullets by a few words (e.g. drop "at maximum perturbation, degradation one third of a VLA's" to one line, or tighten "Long-horizon: one egocentric video drives a 10-minute unseen task"), or reduce the poster row height on this slide. Verify by reshooting this one slide.

### MEDIUM

**Slide 11 — white title over a near-white background** (`tta-2026-11.png`, "Generated worlds became persistent, promptable places")
- The full-bleed Genie 3 poster frame is a white wall; the white two-line title sits directly on it, saved only by its text shadow. On a washed-out projector in a bright room this is the weakest text-on-video contrast in the deck. The other full-bleed titles (07, 15, 19, 23) sit over darker regions and are fine.
- Recommendation: add a scrim (dark gradient band) behind the title on full-bleed video slides, or pick a poster timestamp where the top-left region is darker.

**Slide 26 — interstitial reads as an accidentally empty slide** (`tta-2026-26.png`, "What does a million hours buy?")
- Dark-blue slide with the title and subtitle anchored to the top-left and roughly 75% of the slide empty. The four numbered section dividers (02, 08, 17, 32) all carry a large gold number and vertically centered title, so this unnumbered one breaks the established pattern and can look like a rendering mistake rather than a deliberate breather. Slide 38 ("Appendix") shares the same top-anchored layout, but as the appendix marker it is less likely to be misread.
- Recommendation: vertically center the title block on the unnumbered dark interstitials (or give slide 26 the same divider treatment as the numbered sections).

### LOW

**Slide 15 — in-video frame counter clipped at the left edge** (`tta-2026-15.png`, DreamDojo)
- The left half's "48/600" overlay is partially cut by the slide edge (the "4" is clipped). This is content inside the source video crop, not deck layout; only worth touching if the crop offset is adjustable.

**Slide 20 — figure detail too small for the room** (`tta-2026-20.png`, "Where does action supervision come from?")
- The LAPA poster is a paper figure with large white padding; the actual thumbnails and question text inside it are far too small to read from row 30. The bullets carry the content, so this is decorative — acceptable, but a tighter crop of the figure would use the space better.

**Slide 23 — cream border around the full-bleed video** (`tta-2026-23.png`, Dyna-2 hero reel)
- Unlike the other full-bleed video slides (07, 15, 19, 29, 35), this one shows a visible cream/tan margin and rounded corners around the video on the top, left, and right. The cream does not match the theme's white-to-blue background, so it reads as a stray background color rather than intentional card styling. Cosmetic from distance.

**Slide 25 — table bottom crowds the footnote** (`tta-2026-25.png`, "The race is for hours, not parameters")
- Five two-line rows leave the last cell ("still not enough") nearly touching the bottom rule, with the math footnote directly beneath. No overlap, but zero breathing room; one more line would collide.

**Slide 29 — borderline title contrast** (`tta-2026-29.png`, GEN-1.5)
- White title over a light gray wall and clock. The shadow carries it, but it is the second-weakest title contrast after slide 11. Same scrim fix would cover both.

**Slide 33 — long small footnote pressed against the table** (`tta-2026-33.png`, "Five architectures, one scoreboard")
- The footnote line (NVIDIA ZFLOPs comparison plus quote) is long, small, and sits immediately under the table's bottom rule. Illegible from the room; fine as a footnote, but it visually crowds the table.

**Slide 36 — content runs into the footer margin** (`tta-2026-36.png`, CostNav)
- Same video-plus-bullets layout as slide 43; the last bullet ("Public research from the WoRV team") lands just above the progress bar with no clipping, but there is almost no bottom margin. Any wording growth here reproduces the slide 43 failure.

- **Title, closing, QR:** title slide, bio, QR/thank-you slide, and appendix slides (39-46 body) all render clean; the QR block and URL are large and centered.
