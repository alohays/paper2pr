## Section-Level Flow Analysis

- **Part 0 → Part 1:** Strong opening sequence. The talk establishes the surfer metaphor, earns credibility, and moves into data without wasting time.
- **Part 1 internal flow:** Mostly strong. Slides 4-11 build urgency well, but the job-map and recruiter slides are slightly more "career workshop" in cadence than the surrounding macro-wave slides.
- **Part 1 → Part 2:** Effective. Slides 12-13 create a clean handoff into the Wrtn story, though they perform similar bridging work and could feel slightly redundant in live delivery.
- **Part 2 internal flow:** Very strong. The sequence scale → mechanism → demo → grind → failure → lesson has clear emotional progression and good variety.
- **Part 2 → Part 3:** Intentional and clear. Slide 24 signals the tonal reset well, and the 29%→97.3% teaser gives the new section immediate momentum.
- **Part 3 internal flow:** Strong but denser. Slides 27-33 are intellectually rich, yet the movement from demo to infra to economics slightly flattens energy before recovering on slides 33-35.
- **Part 3 → Part 4:** Best boundary in the deck. Slide 36 explicitly turns the story back toward the audience, which refreshes attention after a long research block.
- **Part 4 internal flow:** Useful and coherent, but this is the main fatigue zone. Slides 44-49 stack several adjacent instructional explanations in a similar cadence, so the section risks sounding more workshop-like than story-like.
- **Part 4 → Part 5:** Mostly successful. Slides 53-56 convert advice into action well, but slide 60 briefly lowers emotional intensity right before the final closing lift.
- **Opening/Closing bookend:** Strong. Slide 1 frames the wave metaphor; slides 61-62 return to "paddle out" and force a concrete decision, so the close does echo the opening effectively.

## Top 5 Flow Improvements

1. Strengthen the bridge from slide 27 to slide 28 so D2E feels like the next logical embodiment example, not a new thread.
2. Add a clearer hinge from slide 31 to slide 32 so research infrastructure and business economics feel causally connected.
3. Vary the opening cadence across slides 44-49; too many adjacent notes use the same explanatory setup, which flattens Part 4 energy.
4. Reuse the `12.7%` retention callback once more in the career-advice block to balance the stronger recurrence of `5M` and `97.3%`.
5. Deliver slide 60 very briskly or frame it as a prelude, so it supports rather than softens the emotional launch into slides 61-62.

## Overall Verdict

- The macro story is coherent and well-designed.
- The strongest narrative asset is the repeated thesis that both product and research success come from defining the right problem and shipping against reality.
- The main weakness is local cadence flattening, not structural confusion.
- The close lands well and meaningfully echoes the opening wave metaphor.
