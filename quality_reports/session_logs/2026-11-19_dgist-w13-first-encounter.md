# W13 opening for first-year students

Screens 07–10 follow a marked front wheel. Students trace one requested turn, locate the motor and encoder, read three quarters of a turn in one second, and choose a floor reference to measure cart travel. The floor and stand pictures are visible while students answer. Screen 02 starts with a warehouse corner. The semester calendar identifies the previous session in its footer.

The only earlier example in the chapter's spoken script is a complete hand-motion command from W06. It follows the introduction of a command using the cart. The separate product comparison and the original W02, W06 and W09 sources are optional instructor material in the Korean guide. The four planned durations are 50, 60, 65 and 75 seconds.

The classroom and standard audience copies were checked in a browser. Both contain 50 slides and omit the presenter notes. The Korean reader was also opened from an offline copy to check the questions, illustrations and glossary references.
