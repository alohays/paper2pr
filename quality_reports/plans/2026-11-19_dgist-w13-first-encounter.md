# W13 opening for a first-time listener

1. Put a marked wheel on screen before naming its parts. Give one complete example from an earlier class as a short spoken aside after the new command makes sense. Keep the course evidence in the instructor references.
2. Follow the same cart through four screens. Show its target, what drives the wheel and senses rotation, the rotation recorded in one second, and the floor reference needed to measure the cart's travel. Move the two product commands to an optional comparison.
3. Match the Korean reader, glossary links, presenter notes and handoff to the revised sequence. Rebuild both audience decks and the offline reader. Inspect the diagrams, questions and reveals at classroom and phone sizes.
