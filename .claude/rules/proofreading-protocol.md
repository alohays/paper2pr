# Proofreading

A copy-edit request authorizes the edit. Keep the author's claim, voice and brief intact. Inspect the affected slide in a fresh build. For a review-only request, return the suggested correction with its slide ID. A change in factual meaning needs the primary-source check in the [shared review workflow](../../.agents/skills/slide-excellence/SKILL.md).
