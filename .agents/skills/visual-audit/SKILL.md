# Inspect audience frames

Use the current `./verify <deck>` output, or `./verify <deck> --slide <id>` for a targeted edit. Check the row furthest from the projector in the declared room. Inspect final fragments, legibility of labels inside figures, poster and caption visibility, the reverse path, and the space reserved for captions in DGIST rooms. Describe each defect by slide ID, viewport and observed behavior. A principal visual review for a new talk or release is independent of its fact and teaching reviews.
